> **Access note (applies to every section).** Research date: 2026-10-19. This session's egress proxy refused direct connections to travelmate.com.br, old.travelmate.com.br, reclameaqui.com.br, web.archive.org and interlatina.org (HTTP 403 policy denial). Third-party scrapers were also unreachable. **So every Travelmate and Reclame Aqui finding below comes from search-engine indexed snippets, summarized by the search tool. None of it was read on the live page.** Wording in quotes is as the search tool returned it and may be paraphrased or translated. Wayback Machine snapshots could not be checked. Instagram and TikTok were not reachable, and no indexed captions about Cascade turned up.

> **Season framing.** Travelmate's Brazilian "Work & Travel" runs during the Brazilian summer holidays. That is the US **winter** season, with program dates between **15 Nov and 15 Mar**. So all of Travelmate's ski-resort Work & Travel content refers to the Dec–Mar season.

## 1. Does Travelmate list Cascade Mountain as a Work & Travel employer? Positions, hourly pay, weekly hours, housing?

### Takeaway
Yes. Travelmate's current Work & Travel program page (travelmate.com.br/programas/work-travel/) has an "Empregadores" (employers) section that lists Cascade Mountain in Portage, Wisconsin. The indexed text gives only a general description of the resort: 47 runs, snowtubing, 30 minutes from Wisconsin Dells and 3 hours from Chicago. **No position, hourly wage, weekly hours or housing cost for Cascade surfaced in any indexed snippet.** No Cascade "Job Description"/"Program Description" PDF was found on Travelmate's server. Travelmate's job offers for other Wisconsin and ski employers give a benchmark: US$12–13/h, about 32 h/week, housing about US$95–100/week.

### Cited Findings
**Cascade Mountain on Travelmate's site (undated program page, indexed 2026):**
- Travelmate's Work & Travel page lists Cascade Mountain as an employer and describes it as "uma estação de ski no coração de Wisconsin" (a ski resort in the heart of Wisconsin) located in Portage, Wisconsin. — [Travelmate – Work & Travel](https://www.travelmate.com.br/programas/work-travel/)
- The same page says Cascade "possui 47 pistas de ski e snowboard, que vão desde o nível iniciante até o avançado". It says snowtubing is also offered and that "a montanha fica a 30 minutos do centro de Wisconsin Dells" and about 3 hours from Chicago. — [Travelmate – Work & Travel](https://www.travelmate.com.br/programas/work-travel/)
- Searches restricted to travelmate.com.br returned **no wage, position, hours or housing data for Cascade**. Several searches came back with the same result: the page names Cascade as an employer but "no wage or position details appear". — [Travelmate – Work & Travel](https://www.travelmate.com.br/programas/work-travel/)
- Travelmate's blog says the Job Offer document is where "a descrição das posições, o valor pago por hora, o nível de inglês necessário e se há ou não moradia" are listed. So Cascade-specific terms would sit in a job-offer document, and none was found indexed. — [Travelmate – Work & Travel](https://www.travelmate.com.br/programas/work-travel/) / [Travelmate blog (category Work & Travel)](https://www.travelmate.com.br/categoria/work-travel/)
- A blog page also says that some employers offer the placement at no cost while others charge a percentage, and that this is stated in the job offer. — [Travelmate – Work & Travel](https://www.travelmate.com.br/programas/work-travel/)

**Other employers on the same Travelmate "Empregadores" list (for context):**
- Mt. Morris, Wisconsin (about 45 min from Fox Valley) and Little Switzerland (founded 1941, Slinger, WI, "one of the oldest ski mountains in Wisconsin"). — [Travelmate – Work & Travel](https://www.travelmate.com.br/programas/work-travel/)
- Also Mt. Olympus Water & Theme Park and Kalahari Resorts (Wisconsin Dells), and Ragged Mountain (Danbury, NH; requires advanced English). — [Travelmate – Work & Travel](https://www.travelmate.com.br/programas/work-travel/)

**Benchmark job offers Travelmate hosts for comparable employers (not Cascade):**
- **Wilderness Hotel & Golf Resort, Wisconsin Dells – Retail Gift Shop Cashier, 2024(-25) season:** pay USD 13.00/h, average 32 h/week. A licensed J-1 housing option costs about US$95/week, plus a US$150 non-refundable administration fee and a US$100 deposit. Benefits include 25% off food and 4 free shuttles. — [Travelmate-hosted PDF: Job Description Wilderness… 2024](https://www.travelmate.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/Job-Description-Wilderness-Hotel-Golf-Resort-Retail-Gift-Shop-Cashier-2024-1.pdf)
- **Wisp Resort (McHenry, Maryland) – J-1 Work & Travel Program Description, 2021-22 season:** Mountain Lift Attendant, Ski Rental Technician and SnowTubing Park/Carpet Attendant all pay "$12.00 per hour (no tips)". Housing rent is $100/week per student. The security deposit is $400, of which $100 is a non-refundable admin fee, paid in cash at orientation; the refundable $300 comes back 4 weeks after departure, and the whole deposit is forfeited on early departure. Transport to the worksite costs $20/week. Perks are an employee F&B discount and free skiing, snowboarding and tubing. — [Travelmate-hosted PDF: Wisp Resort J1 WT Program Description 2021-2022](https://www.travelmate.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/Wisp-Resort-J1-WT-Program-Description-2021-2022.pdf)
- **Older Travelmate program page (undated; the search tool described it as more than 4 years old), Wisp Resort (MD) and June Mountain (CA):** "As funções oferecidas para nossos participantes são de Lift Operator e Rentals. Os salários para estas posições iniciam-se em USD 12 por hora", with about 32 h/week on average. The page adds: "também há possibilidade de second job e overtime." — [old.travelmate.com.br – Work & Travel](https://old.travelmate.com.br/programas/work-and-travel/)

**Cascade terms published by a non-Travelmate agency (labelled):**
- **Interlatina (not Travelmate; not a Brazilian agency), 2023-24 season:** Cascade Mountain Ski Area, Portage WI, Food and Beverage Assistant, $11.00/h, 32 h/week, 6 openings. The search tool noted that the job-description text on that page looked copied from a housekeeping role. — [Interlatina – Cascade Mountain offer](https://interlatina.org/en/work-and-travel/300/offer-work-and-travel-usa-cascade-mountain-ski-area-portage-wi)
- **Snow Season Central (third-party jobs site; page about 989 days old at indexing, so about early 2024):** Cascade hires J-1 students and "uses Intrax to hire most first time students". It says the program is mostly open to students from Central and South America, there is little short-term housing in Portage, and staff without a car may need to live in Wisconsin Dells, paying by the week or month. — [Snow Season Central – Cascade Mountain](https://www.snowseasoncentral.com/usa/cascade-mountain)
- Cascade employs close to 450 people at peak winter season. — [THOR Industries – Behind the scenes at Cascade Mountain](https://www.thorindustries.com/stories/cascade-mountain-ski-resort-family)

### Inferences
- Travelmate's text says 47 runs, while Wikipedia and Travel Wisconsin say 48. That suggests Travelmate's Cascade blurb is generic copy that is not updated each season. It is a marketing description of the employer, not a job offer.
- If Travelmate places students at Cascade, the hourly rate is probably near Interlatina's US$11/h (2023-24, F&B). The other Wisconsin placements Travelmate hosts pay US$12–13/h, with roughly 32 h/week as the typical guaranteed average. Treat this as an estimate, not a Travelmate statement.
- Travelmate's own employer list ties Cascade to the Wisconsin Dells cluster (Wilderness, Kalahari, Mt. Olympus). Students placed there would likely live in Dells-area J-1 housing at about US$95–100/week, as in the Wilderness offer, and would need transport to Portage, 15–30 min away. This is an inference.

### Gaps
- No Cascade-specific Travelmate job offer was found: no positions, US$/h, hours/week, housing cost, deposit or transport. The full live program page could not be read because the domain was blocked, so the page may say more than the snippets show.
- It is unknown in which season Travelmate first or last listed Cascade, because Wayback Machine snapshots could not be checked.

## 2. Program cost (R$ / US$) for recent seasons and which costs are extra

### Takeaway
**No Travelmate program fee in R$ or US$ surfaced** for any 2022-23 to 2026-27 season. Travelmate's only all-in figure is in a student testimonial on its blog: a **total investment of US$3,000–4,000** (date uncertain, indexed about Feb–Mar 2026). Travelmate lists the program inclusions and states that flight, accommodation, SEVIS, passport and visa costs, transport and grace-period insurance are extra. Its guides put **SEVIS at about US$220** and the **visa (MRV) at about US$185**. A promotion prices Work & Travel **visa assistance** (not the program) at **2x R$220 or R$399 à vista**.

### Cited Findings
- **Modalities:** **Premium**: "a oferta de trabalho será providenciada pela TravelMate por meio de seus parceiros nos EUA", described as ideal for first-timers. **Independent**: the participant finds their own job offer, which "o sponsor da TravelMate deverá aprová-la", recommended for advanced English, repeat participants or people with US contacts. — [Travelmate – Work & Travel](https://www.travelmate.com.br/programas/work-travel/)
- **Included:** administrative fees, the DS-2019 form ("necessário para a obtenção do visto J-1"), health insurance during the work period, a pre-departure orientation meeting, a 24-hour emergency phone line in the US, and Travelmate/sponsor assistance in the US. — [Travelmate – Work & Travel](https://www.travelmate.com.br/programas/work-travel/)
- **Not included:** airfare, accommodation, SEVIS fees, passport and visa expenses, and transport. Health insurance during the 30-day Grace Period is also not included. — [Travelmate – Work & Travel](https://www.travelmate.com.br/programas/work-travel/)
- **SEVIS:** "aproximadamente USD 220 (sujeito a alterações)", paid on the DHS site after receiving the DS-2019. **Visa (MRV):** "Aproximadamente USD 185". (Guide titled "Documentos para Camp Work nos EUA: Guia Completo 2025" exists on the same site, and the W&T guide appears to be from the same 2025 period.) — [Travelmate – Work & Travel EUA: Guia de Documentos e Visto J-1](https://www.travelmate.com.br/blog/documentos-work-travel-eua/)
- **Total investment (Travelmate blog testimonial):** "ter em mente que o investimento total é entre US$ 3.000,00 e 4.000,00." The testimonial says that "além do pacote da agência" the student pays for: "passaporte (se ainda não tem); passagem de avião; visto e custo da viagem até uma cidade com consulado americano", plus rent, a deposit for the first weeks, and other costs. The indexer dated the post about 229–230 days before 2026-10-19 (about Feb–Mar 2026), which may be a last-modified date. — [Travelmate – Depoimento Work & Travel](https://www.travelmate.com.br/blog/depoimento-work-travel/)
- **Visa-assistance promotion:** "2x R$220 no cartão ou R$399 à vista" for Work & Travel visa assistance, "Não incluso: taxas consulares", "Válido para inscrições em julho de 2026". The search tool flagged page metadata of about 8 years, so the date is inconsistent and the price should be confirmed. — [Travelmate – Promoções](https://www.travelmate.com.br/promocoes/)
- **Program dates:** duration of 3–4 months set by the employer. "As datas do programa devem estar compreendidas entre 15 de novembro a 15 de março do ano seguinte", followed by 30 days of travel (Grace Period). — [Travelmate – Work & Travel](https://www.travelmate.com.br/programas/work-travel/)
- **Eligibility:** age 18–28, intermediate English, enrolled in an in-person undergraduate course (no distance learning or technical courses), last semester between August and December. — [Travelmate – Work & Travel](https://www.travelmate.com.br/programas/work-travel/)
- **Employer-side costs (benchmark):** housing deposit and fees from the Wilderness 2024 offer (US$150 non-refundable plus US$100 deposit) and the Wisp 2021-22 offer (US$400 deposit, US$100 of it non-refundable; US$20/week transport). — [Wilderness 2024 PDF](https://www.travelmate.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/Job-Description-Wilderness-Hotel-Golf-Resort-Retail-Gift-Shop-Cashier-2024-1.pdf); [Wisp 2021-22 PDF](https://www.travelmate.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/Wisp-Resort-J1-WT-Program-Description-2021-2022.pdf)
- **Third-party market reference (NOT Travelmate):** Universidade do Intercâmbio estimates a total pre-departure cost of R$9,000–15,000, including a program fee of R$3,000–6,000. — [Universidade do Intercâmbio – Work and Travel EUA: quanto custa](https://www.universidadedointercambio.com/work-and-travel-eua-quanto-custa-e-como-se-inscrever/)

### Inferences
- The scope of the US$3,000–4,000 testimonial figure is ambiguous. "Investimento total" suggests everything. The search tool, however, read the following "além do pacote da agência…" list as costs on top of that figure. The report should present it as "Travelmate testimonial: total investment US$3–4k (scope unclear)".
- Adding Travelmate's own extras (SEVIS about US$220 plus MRV about US$185) to a Brazil–Chicago flight puts out-of-pocket pre-departure costs well above the program fee. The first rent and deposit on arrival (about US$250–400) come on top.

### Gaps
- No Travelmate program fee (Premium or Independent) was found in R$ or US$ for 2022-23, 2023-24, 2024-25, 2025-26 or 2026-27. The live pages and Wayback copies could not be read. A direct quote from Travelmate or an archived price page is needed.
- It is unclear whether insurance during work is bundled in the fee or charged separately. Travelmate says it is "incluso", but no amount is given.

## 4. General Travelmate claims on how much a Work & Travel participant earns or saves in a winter season

### Takeaway
Travelmate's published figures are modest and hedged. Hourly pay is "normalmente entre 7,25 e 12 dólares por hora", while job-specific offers run US$12–13/h with about 32 h/week average and about 40 h/week in good cases. Overtime is 1.5x above 40 h, tips can exceed the hourly wage in some roles, and second jobs are possible. Its central savings claim is that a student earning about US$12/h for about 40 h/week "provavelmente terá juntado todo o valor das despesas" (recovers all costs) by season's end. No Travelmate statement of a typical net amount saved per winter season was found.

### Cited Findings
- "A média salarial fica normalmente entre 7,25 e 12 dólares por hora, variando conforme empregador e função." Pay is calculated per hour worked, and the rate depends on company and role. — [Travelmate blog – Work and Travel Estados Unidos](https://www.travelmate.com.br/blog/work-and-travel-estados-unidos/) / [Travelmate – 6 programas para trabalhar no exterior](https://www.travelmate.com.br/blog/6-programas-de-intercambio-para-trabalhar-no-exterior/)
- The US federal minimum wage is $7.25/h (Travelmate destination page, about 590 days old at indexing, so about March 2025). — [Travelmate – Estados Unidos](https://www.travelmate.com.br/destinos/estados-unidos/)
- "Os salários para estas posições iniciam-se em USD 12 por hora… também há possibilidade de second job e overtime", with about 32 h/week average (old page, undated). — [old.travelmate – Work & Travel](https://old.travelmate.com.br/programas/work-and-travel/)
- Wilderness 2024: US$13/h, 32 h/week average. Wisp 2021-22: US$12/h, no tips. — [Wilderness 2024 PDF](https://www.travelmate.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/Job-Description-Wilderness-Hotel-Golf-Resort-Retail-Gift-Shop-Cashier-2024-1.pdf); [Wisp 2021-22 PDF](https://www.travelmate.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/Wisp-Resort-J1-WT-Program-Description-2021-2022.pdf)
- Savings/break-even claim: about 40 h/week at about US$12/h means you "provavelmente terá juntado todo o valor das despesas no final da temporada". Tips can exceed hourly pay, and overtime is paid at 1.5x. — [Travelmate – Depoimento Work & Travel](https://www.travelmate.com.br/blog/depoimento-work-travel/)
- Having housing and/or food from the employer gives "alguns dólares de vantagem". This comes from a snippet in a travelmate.com.br-restricted search, and the exact page was not identified, so confidence is low. — [Travelmate – Work & Travel](https://www.travelmate.com.br/programas/work-travel/)
- **Other agencies (labelled, NOT Travelmate):** IE Intercâmbio (2026) gives US$8–14/h for Work and Travel. — [IE Intercâmbio – Work and Travel: como funciona e quanto paga em 2026](https://www.ie.com.br/intercambio/work-and-travel/)

### Inferences
- Simple arithmetic from Travelmate's own benchmarks, as an illustration and not a Travelmate claim: US$12–13/h × 32 h × about 13–16 weeks gives roughly US$5,000–6,650 gross. Housing at about US$95–100/week takes about US$1,300–1,600 of that, before food, transport and US taxes. This fits Travelmate's framing that the season roughly pays back a US$3–4k investment rather than producing large savings.
- Travelmate's "7,25–12 USD/h" range is outdated against its own 2024 offer of US$13/h and against Wisconsin ski wages. The report should label it as legacy copy.

### Gaps
- No Travelmate figure for typical net savings per winter (for example, "volta com US$ X") was found for any season since 2022-23.
- The dates of the blog texts are uncertain because indexer "age" metadata may reflect updates, not first publication.

## 5. Which J-1 sponsor does Travelmate use (for Cascade placements)?

### Takeaway
Travelmate's pages refer only to "o sponsor da TravelMate" without naming it in indexed snippets. External evidence points to **Spirit Cultural Exchange**, which names TravelMate as its "International Partner" and ran a 2026 J-1 Work & Travel job fair in Brazil with it. **InterExchange** lists "Travelmate & Tourism Exchange Ltd" as a Brazil partner agency for Work & Travel USA. **Which sponsor covers Cascade placements specifically is unconfirmed.** A third-party jobs site says Cascade recruits most first-time J-1 students through **Intrax**, and no Intrax–Travelmate link was found.

### Cited Findings
- Travelmate: the Independent modality job offer "o sponsor da TravelMate deverá aprová-la". "O DS-2019 é o documento emitido pelo sponsor". — [Travelmate – Work & Travel](https://www.travelmate.com.br/programas/work-travel/); [Travelmate – Guia de Documentos e Visto J-1](https://www.travelmate.com.br/documentos-work-travel-eua/)
- Spirit Cultural Exchange's Work Abroad page describes TravelMate as its International Partner and mentions a 2026 J-1 Work and Travel job fair in Brazil organized together. Spirit is a designated BridgeUSA J-1 sponsor for Work and Travel. — [Spirit Cultural Exchange – Work Abroad](https://www.spiritexchange.com/travel/)
- InterExchange's Work & Travel USA partner-agency list includes Brazil cooperator "Travelmate & Tourism Exchange Ltd" (page about 363 days old at indexing). — [InterExchange – Partner Agencies](https://www.interexchange.org/programs/work-travel-usa/international-staff/partner-agencies/)
- For Au Pair, Travelmate's partners are InterExchange (Au Pair USA) and AuPairCare, part of the Intrax group. That is a different program. Camp Work runs through InterExchange's platform. — [Travelmate – Au Pair](https://www.travelmate.com.br/programas/au-pair/); [Travelmate – Camp Work](https://www.travelmate.com.br/programas/camp-work/)
- Cascade "uses Intrax to hire most first time students" (third-party page, about early 2024). — [Snow Season Central – Cascade Mountain](https://www.snowseasoncentral.com/usa/cascade-mountain)

### Inferences
- If Travelmate sends Premium participants to Cascade, the DS-2019 most likely comes from Spirit Cultural Exchange or InterExchange, not Intrax. Cascade may take students from several sponsors: Intrax for "most first-timers", plus others such as the Interlatina-linked sponsor in 2023-24. This is unverified.

### Gaps
- No document names the sponsor on a Travelmate Cascade offer. The Wilderness and Wisp PDFs' sponsor lines were not visible in snippets.

## 6. Reclame Aqui complaints/reviews about Travelmate Work & Travel mentioning earnings, hours or Cascade Mountain
